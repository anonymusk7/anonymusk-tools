FAQ

Are the slides really editable?

Yes. Text, shapes and charts are native PowerPoint objects, not flattened images, so they open and edit in PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides.

Do you write the numbers for me?

No. We format and visualize the numbers you supply. We never invent traction, TVL or holder counts. The template pack ships with clearly marked [PLACEHOLDERS].

Can you match my brand?

Yes. Send your logo, colors and fonts. If you don't have a brand yet, we'll suggest one that fits your chain and audience.
