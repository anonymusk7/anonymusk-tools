FAQ

Do you need our brand assets?

A logo (SVG or PNG) and palette help. If you don't send any, we pull what's public from your site and X and confirm with you first.

Can you use live price / holder data?

Yes. Stat clips use free public sources (DexScreener, CoinGecko, DefiLlama), and we snapshot the numbers at render time and show you which ones we used.

Do you make price predictions or "100x" claims?

No. We don't put financial-advice claims or invented numbers in videos. Everything on screen has to match your public data or your own copy.

How do I pay?

After you approve the quote we send an invoice. Nothing is charged on this page.
