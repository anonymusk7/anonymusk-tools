Motion prompt gallery

22 copy-paste prompts for Claude/Opus + Remotion. Every preview below was rendered with Remotion.

Want the production versions — real data hooks, vertical + landscape, brand theming? Get the Crypto Motion Pack ($29) →
#1 Kinetic type

Animate the token ticker in heavy sans; letter-spacing collapses from 60px to 0 over 40 frames with fade-in, then hold. 1080x1080, 30fps, dark grid background drifting diagonally.

#2 Equalizer bars

12 rounded bars with staggered sine-wave heights (phase 0.9 per bar, 3 rad/s), hue shifting per bar. Use as a volume/hype beat behind stats.

#3 Pulse ring

A neon ring that breathes (scale 1±0.2 at 4 rad/s) with a glow whose blur tracks the pulse. Center the logo inside for a launch countdown.

#4 Odometer counter

Count 0→1,000,000 with sqrt easing over 2s, tabular numbers, thousands separators. Swap in holders/TVL/volume.

#5 Line chart draw

Draw a price line left-to-right over 2.5s, stroke 10px, accent color; then pin a label at the last point with the % change.

#6 Orbit tokens

12 rounded squares orbiting an ellipse (300x170) at 1 rad/s; replace with partner logos for an ecosystem shot.

#7 Wipe reveal

Reveal a headline with a left-to-right clip-path wipe at 3%/frame; follow with a subtitle fade 10 frames later.

#8 Grid cascade

4x3 grid of tiles springs up from +300px with a 3-frame stagger; each tile becomes a feature card.

#9 Glitch title

Title with RGB split text-shadow (6px red/cyan) and deterministic horizontal jitter every 9 frames for the first 1.5s, then lock.

#10 Progress bar

Gradient progress bar fills at 40%/s; label it 'mainnet sync' or 'raise filled' with a % readout.

#11 3D spin

Logo mark rotates on Y at 120°/s with 800px perspective; end facing camera on the brand color.

#12 Launch stinger

Combine #1 + #3: ticker kinetic type inside a pulse ring, cut to price odometer at 1.5s.

#13 Listing card

Exchange listing: 'NOW LIVE ON <CEX>' wipe reveal, pair badge, then line-chart draw of first hour.

#14 Holder milestone

Odometer to the milestone, confetti of tiles from grid cascade, vertical 1080x1920.

#15 Partnership

Two logos orbit then snap together center with a pulse; 'x' glyph glitch-in between them.

#16 TVL weekly

Equalizer bars as daily TVL (7 bars), real numbers from DefiLlama, label the max day.

#17 Airdrop teaser

Glitch title 'SEASON 2', progress bar 'points snapshot', countdown odometer to the date.

#18 Roadmap

Grid cascade of quarters; each tile lights up in sequence with a progress bar underneath.

#19 Burn counter

Odometer counting tokens burned, red hue, embers = orbit particles drifting upward.

#20 Chart recap

Line draw for 7d, overlay pulse ring on ATH point, end with % change in kinetic type.

#21 Countdown

3-2-1 in 3D spin numerals, each beat a pulse ring, last beat cuts to logo wipe reveal.

#22 Thread to clip

Each tweet line of a thread becomes a wipe-revealed card in a grid cascade, 4s per card.
